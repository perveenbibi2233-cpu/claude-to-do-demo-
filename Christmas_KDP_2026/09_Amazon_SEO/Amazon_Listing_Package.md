## 8. Suggested list price
**Recommendation: US $11.99** for the paperback (try a range of $10.99–$12.99).

Reasoning (verify each number in KDP's pricing calculator before you publish; these are estimates, not guarantees):
- **Print cost:** KDP's help page gives premium colour as $1.00 fixed + $0.065 per page for longer books. Third-party guides report a flat fee of about $3.60 for 24–40-page premium-colour books at regular trim. 8.5 in wide counts as a **large trim**, which can cost more. **Expect roughly $3.60–$4.25 per copy.** KDP's calculator will show the exact figure.
- **Royalty:** 60% of list price minus print cost for paperbacks priced at $9.99 and above (lower-priced paperbacks earn a reduced rate; confirm the current rule). At $11.99: 0.60 × 11.99 = $7.19 − ~$3.60–4.25 ≈ **$2.95–$3.60 per sale**.
- **Competitive context:** self-published square Christmas picture-book paperbacks are usually priced in the ~$9.99–$12.99 band, and traditionally published hardcovers usually ~$17–$19. Check this against 10–15 current comparable listings on launch day. $11.99 sits mid-band: it signals quality without beating the bigger brands on price.
- **Hardcover:** KDP hardcovers don't offer an 8.5 × 8.5 trim (check the current list). A hardcover would need a new layout at a supported size. That's optional for later.

## 9. Positioning and marketing strategy
**Positioning statement:** *The cozy Christmas Eve bedtime book with a brave little hedgehog. A gift that gets read every night in December.*

**Launch timeline (Christmas 2026; today is 4 Nov):**
1. **Now – 10 Nov:** finalize the author name, order **one author proof copy**, and check print colour (navy skies can print darker; brighten if needed).
2. **By ~14 Nov:** publish the paperback. Review can take up to 72 hours. Leave margin for US shipping cut-offs in mid-December.
3. **Optional:** a Kindle edition through Kindle Kids' Book Creator (fixed-layout) using the same art. It lets readers "Look Inside" for longer.
4. **Look Inside / A+ Content:** use the spot illustrations and the character sheet to build A+ modules ("Meet Juniper & friends", "A refrain to read together", "Discussion questions inside").
5. **Amazon Ads:** start small with automatic targeting plus manual keyword campaigns on the seven phrases above. Add product targeting on similar *self-published* Christmas animal picture books. Cap the daily budget and review CPC weekly. Expect December CPCs to rise.
6. **Off-Amazon:** short read-aloud videos of the refrain (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts), outreach to teachers ("discussion questions at the back"), local library and bookshop holiday story times, and a free printable "Little Light" colouring page as a lead magnet.
7. **Series:** title the next books "Juniper and the..." (spring, birthday, first snow day) to build a brand for Christmas 2027.

**Claims to avoid:** "bestseller", "award-winning", "#1", or any implied endorsement or affiliation with Santa-themed brands or other books.
