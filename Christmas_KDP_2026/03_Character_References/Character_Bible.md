# Character Bible: *Juniper and the Little Lost Star*

Visual reference: `character_sheet.png` (all characters, poses and colour swatches, rendered from the same code as the book).

**How consistency is enforced.** Each character is drawn by exactly one function in `_build/art.py`: `hedgehog()`, `glimmer()`, `robin()`, `fox()` and `owl()`. Scenes can change only pose, expression, gaze, scale and position. Proportions, palette, clothing and facial geometry are fixed in code, so they can't drift between pages.

---

## Juniper (main character)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Species / age** | Young hedgehog, about 5 in human terms (relatable to readers aged 3–7) |
| **Body proportions** | About 2 heads tall. Egg-shaped body, large round head merged with the body. Short oval arms, small oval feet. Height-to-width about 1.3 : 1. |
| **Face** | Large cream face mask. Two big glossy black oval eyes with a large white highlight (upper right) and a small one (lower left). Pale round snout with a black button nose. Small smile. Rosy oval cheeks. |
| **Fur / spikes** | Chocolate-brown spikes (#6B4430) with caramel tips (#B07F57) forming a halo around the head and sides. **Signature:** a 3-spike lighter tuft on top of her head. |
| **Ears** | Small, round, beige, with pink insides (#F2A79F) |
| **Clothing** | Holly-red knitted scarf (#C8303A) with **two white stripes** and fringe on the tail, which hangs on *her* left (the viewer's right). She doesn't wear it on pp. 5–6 (before the gift) or pp. 18–25 (it's with Glimmer). She gets it back on p. 26. |
| **Personality** | Curious, kind, brave in spite of feeling small, a bit wobbly when things get scary, and funny without meaning to be |
| **Signature line** | "Little light, little light, don't you fade tonight." |
| **Palette** | #6B4430 · #B07F57 · #F6DFBC · #F2A79F · #F2948A · #C8303A · #FFF7EE · #2B1B14 |

## Glimmer (the little lost star)
| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Hollyberry Village's own star, the one Santa follows |
| **Shape** | Five-pointed star with softly rounded points, about 1/3 of Juniper's height |
| **Face** | Two small black oval eyes, tiny smile, pink cheeks. Sad: tilted brows, small frown, one tear. Surprised: round mouth. |
| **Colour** | Golden gradient (#FFF4B8 → #FFD54A → #F5A623) with a soft halo. **Her glow tracks her feelings:** bright when loved and warm, pale (#F3E3A6) and dim when cold or afraid. |
| **Personality** | Sweet, giggly, anxious when far from home, deeply grateful |

## Grandma Hedgehog
Same build as Juniper, with silver-grey spikes (#8E8079 / #CBBFB3), round brown spectacles and a lavender knitted shawl (#8E7CC3). She's warm and wise and knits all winter. She has a "knowing smile".

## Ruby the robin
A plump, round robin: brown back (#7A5236), bright orange-red breast and face (#E8582F), golden beak, bead eye. Chirpy, eager and a little over-confident.

## Ember the fox
A friendly red fox, always drawn in side view: orange coat (#DB6A2A / #F29A55), white chest and muzzle, dark-brown socks and ear tips, and a huge bushy tail with a white tip ("a tail like a torch"). Gentle and gallant, the group's "big brother".

## Old Oakley the owl
A large round grey owl (#8A8EA3), pale lavender-grey belly with chevron markings, cream face discs, **big amber "moon" eyes** (#F4B42F), white brow tufts and ear tufts. Calm, wise, kind.

## World: Hollyberry Village
Snowy cottages with warm gold windows (#FFD27A), snow-capped roofs in red, blue, green and plum, a clock tower, pine woods, and the old holly tree with Juniper's round wooden burrow door. Night palette: #0A1736 → #1C3566 → #3F5F95. Snow: #F4F8FF → #8FA7CF.
