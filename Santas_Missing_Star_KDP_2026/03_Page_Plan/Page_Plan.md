## Pages 2–3: The Missing Star

**Story text (left page, top-left panel):**

> It was Christmas Eve at the North Pole, and the whole village sparkled.
> Santa looked up at his enormous Christmas tree and gasped. “Oh my snowflakes! My golden star is gone!”
> Pip the little fox stepped forward. “Don't worry, Santa. We'll find it!”
> “We'll help too!” said Mimi, Bramble and Tinsel.

> *Look for clues on every page, and find all the hidden objects along the way!*
> *Psst! Our friends' own scarves, hats, jumpers and bells don't count.*

**Illustration:** Santa's snowy Christmas village at dusk: candy-coloured cottages with glowing windows, Santa's enormous Christmas tree in the town square, dressed in garlands and warm fairy lights (no baubles yet), with an EMPTY treetop (no star). Right page: Santa stands beside the tree, hands on his cheeks in surprise, looking up at the empty top. Pip stands in front of Santa, chest out, brave and determined, with Mimi, Bramble and Tinsel gathered close behind him looking up. Left page: the village street leading to the square, lantern light, gentle falling snow.

**Characters:** Santa, Pip, Mimi, Bramble, Tinsel

## Pages 10–11: Snowflake Forest

**Story text (left page, top-left panel):**

> The friends tiptoed into Snowflake Forest. Snow sparkled on every branch.

**Illustration:** A magical snowy forest of tall birch and fir trunks with snow-laden branches, soft blue-violet shadows, a winding path, a little frozen stream, gentle soft-dot snowfall. **Characters:** Pip, Mimi, Bramble, Tinsel

## Pages 20–21: Northern Lights Hill

**Story text (left page, top-left panel):**

> Up on Northern Lights Hill, the sky danced green and purple.
> Two little rabbits were fast asleep in the snow. “Shh!” whispered Mimi.
> On the hilltop lay one more golden feather, pointing back toward town!

**Search panel (right page, bottom-right):** “Can you find all five hidden objects? Look carefully!”

- 5 stars: *The ONLY stars: five big golden stars. The sky has aurora ribbons but no other star dots.* (placed from sticker sheet)
- 3 pine trees: *Exactly three pine trees on the hill.* (painted in scene, verified by hand)
- 4 snowflakes: *Large sparkly snowflake shapes.* (placed from sticker sheet)
- 2 sleeping rabbits: *Brown wild rabbits curled up asleep. Mimi is awake and doesn't count.* (placed from sticker sheet)
- 1 golden feather (placed from sticker sheet)

**Illustration:** A rounded snowy hilltop at night under swirling green and purple northern lights (aurora ribbons only, no star dots in the sky), the glowing village far below, EXACTLY THREE pine trees on the hill. Mimi with a finger to her lips; Pip pointing at the hilltop; Bramble tiptoeing exaggeratedly; Tinsel gazing at the sky.

**Characters:** Pip, Mimi, Bramble, Tinsel

## Pages 24–25: Goldie's Secret

**Story text (left page, top-left panel):**

> In the branches of the fir tree sat a tiny nest. Bramble lifted Pip up to see.
> Inside was a tiny golden bird, and beside her, safe and sound, was Santa's golden star!
> “Did you take the star?” Pip asked gently.
> “Oh no,” peeped the bird. “I'm Goldie. The wind blew the star off the tree. I flew all over the village looking for a safe place to keep it, and I lost a few feathers on the way!”

**Illustration:** Close, warm scene in a snowy fir tree at evening: a cozy nest of twigs and moss on a sturdy branch. Inside the nest sits tiny Goldie, and beside her the big shining golden five-pointed star, glowing softly. Bramble stands below holding Pip up on his shoulders so Pip is eye-level with the nest; Pip looks at Goldie with a gentle, kind face. Mimi and Tinsel watch from below. A few loose golden feathers on the branch.

**Characters:** Pip, Bramble, Mimi, Tinsel, Goldie

## Pages 26–27: The Christmas Celebration

**Story text (left page, top-left panel):**

> Santa smiled his warmest smile. “Thank you, Goldie. You kept my star safe, and that was very kind.”
> Santa lifted Pip high, and Pip placed the star on top of the tree. It glowed brighter than ever!
> “Will you join our celebration, Goldie?” asked Santa. “Yes, please!” she chirped.
> And the whole village cheered.

**Illustration:** Joyful celebration in the town square at night: the enormous Christmas tree now topped with the shining golden star. Santa holds Pip up high near the treetop as Pip sets the star in place; Goldie flutters beside them. Below, Mimi, Bramble and Tinsel hang the last decorations and cheer; warm lights, gentle snow, glowing cottage windows.

**Characters:** Santa, Pip, Goldie, Mimi, Bramble, Tinsel
