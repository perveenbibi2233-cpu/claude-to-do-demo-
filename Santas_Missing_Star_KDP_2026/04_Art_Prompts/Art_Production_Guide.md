# Art Production Guide

**Who does what:** you make the pictures with your image tool. I check them, place the hidden objects, set the text, count everything, and build the KDP files.

## 1. Order of work
1. **C1, C2, C3:** Santa and Goldie reference sheets, and Tinsel remade with her rosy-pink nose. Send them to me so I can check them against your other sheets before any scene uses them.
2. **O1–O5:** the five sticker sheets (the hidden objects).
3. **S1–S13:** the 13 two-page scenes. Send a few at a time. I'll flag any character drift early.
4. **T1–T3 and K1–K2:** title page, spot-the-difference picture, closing page, front and back covers.

Full list with file names: `Art_Checklist.md`. Prompts: `Art_Prompt_Pack.md`.

## 2. Always attach the reference sheets
Every prompt lists which sheets to attach. Attach them every time, even for a small fix. That's what keeps Pip's scarf, Mimi's hat and Tinsel's spots the same on every page.

## 3. Sizes, and why upscaling matters
KDP prints at 300 DPI. Your tool makes 1536 × 1024 images, but a two-page scene needs **5175 × 3375 px**:

| Picture | Your tool's shape | Print size needed |
|---|---|---|
| Two-page scene | 3:2 (1536 × 1024) | 5175 × 3375 px (17.25 × 11.25 in incl. bleed) |
| Single page | 2:3 (1024 × 1536) | 2588 × 3375 px (8.625 × 11.25 in) |
| Full cover | front + back art | 5198 × 3375 px (17.3251 × 11.25 in) |

**Upscale every image 4× before you send it.** [Upscayl](https://upscayl.org) is free, open-source and runs on your computer; the "Digital Art" model suits this style. 1536 × 1024 becomes 6144 × 4096, which I crop to the exact print size.
If you can't upscale, send the originals anyway. I'll build a proof from them, but those pages will print soft and KDP may warn about low resolution.

## 4. How the hidden objects stay exact
Image tools are bad at exact counts. Ask for "3 teddy bears" and you often get 2 or 5. So for most targets:
- The **scene** prompts tell the tool to leave those objects *out*.
- The **sticker sheets** give me each object drawn separately on white.
- **I place exactly the right number** of each object into the scene, matched to its lighting and scale, away from the binding and the text panels.

Because I place them, I know every position, so the answer key is exact.

One target belongs to the scene itself and can't be a sticker: the 3 pine trees on pages 20–21. That prompt asks for exactly three, and I count them by hand.

If a scene arrives with an extra one, or with a target object it was told to leave out, I'll tell you which picture to regenerate.

## 5. What I check on every picture you send
- Characters match their sheets: face, colours, clothing, markings and size.
- No extra characters, text, letters or watermarks.
- The binding strip and text areas are clear.
- No stray copies of that scene's hidden objects.

Anything that fails comes back to you with the reason and a corrected prompt.
