# Character Lock: Where Is Santa's Missing Star?

Reference sheets you supplied are in `reference_sheets/`. Santa and Goldie need sheets made first (prompts C1, C2). Tinsel's sheet is remade with her new rosy-pink nose (prompt C3); the original red-nose sheets are kept for reference only and must not be attached to scene prompts.

## Pip
small orange-red fox cub (the hero, our height reference), rounded soft body, large rounded head, large warm amber-brown eyes, upright triangular ears with dark brown tips and cream insides, cream muzzle and chest, tiny dark brown nose, dark brown lower legs and paws, big fluffy orange-red tail with cream-white tip, teal knitted scarf with small white snowflake motifs wrapped twice around the neck with two fringed ends on the chest. No other clothing.

Reference: `Pip_fox.png`

## Mimi
petite fluffy round white rabbit, about two-thirds of Pip's height, large violet-brown eyes, long upright white ears with pink insides, tiny pink triangular nose, small round tail, lavender cable-knit winter hat with a round lavender pom-pom, ears poking up through the hat. No other clothing.

Reference: `Mimi_rabbit.png`

## Bramble
round, sturdy chestnut-brown bear cub, a little taller and wider than Pip, small round ears, honey-brown eyes, cream muzzle, dark brown nose, rounded paws, bright red knitted Christmas jumper with a large white snowflake on the chest and white Nordic pattern bands. No hat or scarf.

Reference: `Bramble_bear.png`

## Tinsel
small, slightly plump caramel-brown baby reindeer, about Pip's shoulder height, cream muzzle and chest, small cream spots on her back, large dark brown eyes with lashes, tiny symmetrical light-brown branching antlers, soft rosy-pink round nose (rosy pink, NOT red), dark chocolate hooves, fluffy cream-tipped tail, red collar with one small golden bell. No other accessories.

Reference: `Tinsel_reference_pink_nose.png (make it first, prompt C3)`

## Santa
kind elderly man (about 65–70), tall and broad with a gentle round belly, rosy cheeks, warm dark blue eyes, thick fluffy white eyebrows, full fluffy white beard to his upper chest, white hair at the sides, pinkish-red round nose, bright red coat and trousers with white fur trim, red Santa hat with white pom-pom, wide black belt with golden buckle, black boots, white gloves.

Reference: `Santa_reference.png (make it first, prompt C1)`

## Goldie
a very tiny round fluffy golden-yellow bird (small enough to sit in Pip's front paws), buttery-yellow feather highlights, large sparkling dark brown eyes, small orange-yellow beak, short rounded wings, three short golden tail feathers, tiny orange feet, a small feather crest. No clothing.

Reference: `Goldie_reference.png (make it first, prompt C2)`

## Size ladder (shortest → tallest)
Goldie (fits in Pip's paws) → Mimi (⅔ of Pip) → Tinsel (to Pip's shoulder, antlers above) → Pip → Bramble (a little taller and wider) → Santa (Pip reaches below his knee).
