# KDP Specifications: Where Is Santa's Missing Star?

The figures below come from KDP's help pages, read on 4 Nov 2026. Check them again in KDP's cover calculator when you upload.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Trim | 8.5 × 11 in, portrait |
| Interior | Premium Color, white paper, **bleed on** |
| Page count | 32 (KDP paperback minimum is 24) |
| Interior PDF page size | **8.625 × 11.25 in** (0.125 in bleed on top, bottom and outside edge) |
| Two-page scene art | 17.25 × 11.25 in = **5175 × 3375 px** at 300 DPI |
| Single-page art | 8.625 × 11.25 in = **2588 × 3375 px** |
| Margins used | text ≥ 0.75 in inside trim (KDP minimum: 0.375 in outside and 0.375 in gutter for 24–150 pages) |
| Binding keep-out | no faces, text or hidden objects within 0.75 in of the fold |
| Spine | 32 × 0.002347 in = **0.0751 in**; no spine text (KDP prints spine text only over 79 pages) |
| Full cover | 0.125 + 8.5 + 0.0751 + 8.5 + 0.125 = **17.3251 × 11.25 in** (5198 × 3375 px) |
| Barcode | KDP places a 2 × 1.2 in box at the lower right of the back cover. Keep it clear. |
| Fonts | Andika and Fredoka (SIL Open Font License), embedded |

**Print cost.** Premium colour at an 8.5 in width counts as a large trim. Look up the exact figure in KDP's pricing calculator rather than estimating it.
