## 0. How to use this pack

1. Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** at the end of every scene prompt.
2. When a scene names a character, paste that character's **BASE LOOK** plus the outfit code (for example `Yuki + Outfit Y2`). The face stays the same and only the clothes change.
3. Generate the image first, then animate it (Kling, Hailuo, Pika and so on) using the **Motion** line.
4. Aspect ratio is 16:9. Each scene is a 5–8 second clip, so 80 scenes make about 8–10 minutes.

## 1. The story

**Logline:** Three young women in three cold countries are online pen-pals. On the first snow day of the year they make a promise: each will film how her country spends winter, then mail the others a "winter box". As the video moves from Japan to Korea to Finland, the audience sees three different ways to stay warm. Each country has its own warmth: Japan's kotatsu, Korea's heated ondol floor and Finland's sauna.

**Structure (80 scenes)**

| Part | Scenes | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 1–6 | The first snow falls in all three countries and the video-call promise is made |
| Japan: "Warmth Under the Table" | 7–28 | Hokkaido village: kotatsu, onsen, snow hut, New Year's food, shrine visit |
| Korea: "Warmth From the Floor" | 29–50 | Seoul: ondol, kimjang with grandma, street food, jjimjilbang, study café |
| Finland: "Warmth in the Dark" | 51–72 | Lapland: polar night, sauna and ice swim, reindeer, aurora, candles |
| Ending: "One Winter" | 73–80 | The winter boxes arrive, each tries the others' traditions, final call |

**Emotional thread:** each girl has a quiet winter loneliness that her elderly neighbour helps heal. Yuki's grandfather has passed away, Seo-yeon is a stressed student living alone, and Aino faces 50 days with no sunrise. The ending message: *"Winter is cold everywhere. Warmth is something people make together."*
